### What is the Iliad

Basically, ancient Greek Mythology. Written by a guy named Homer. The Iliad focuses on Achilles and the events of the Trojan War.

We only cover 4 books in this class:
- **Book 1**: Achilles and Agamemnon kidnaps 2 girls as slaves. Father of one of the girl tries to ransom the girl back, but Agamemnon refuses, so he asks Apollo to kill a bunch of Greeks. Agamenon reluctantly returns the girl, but steals Achilles'. Achilles gets mad, asks his mom to ask Zeus to take revenge for him.
- **Book 18**: Achilles is informed of Patroclus' death. Hector steals Achilles' armor. Achilles pull up and frightens the Trojan soldiers, allowing the Greek to retrieve Patroclus' body. Hephaestus makes Achilles some really good equipment.
- **Book 22**: Achilles chases Apollo, thinking him someone else, until he realizes he is deceived, then runs back to Troy. The Trojans hide inside their walls, but Hector refuses to and stays outside to fight Achilles instead. Hectors then is frightened by Achilles and flees, until Athena transforms into his brother to deceive him, convincing him to face Achilles head on. Achilles kills Hector. The people grieve.
- **Book 24**: The gods convinces Achilles to give back Hector's body. Hermes escorts Priam to Achilles to ransom back the body. Achilles promises he will not attack for 12 days to let the Trojans mourn.

### The Text

I opted to read the Iliad first instead of the Hebrew Bible, cause obviously I was more excited about it.  
I don't want to say that I am disappointed, because I will come back to this one in the future to finish it in its entirety, but for now, let's just say that it's not what I expected. I hope it was because of the time constraints, and not because I overhyped it for myself.

Alot of people say visualizing a book yourself with your own imagination is always better than seeing someone else' intepretation. I don't think it's true in my case. Had I not played Hades, I probably would've enjoyed this alot less.

Compared to Gilgamesh, where fights usually last like a paragraph, Iliad fights are much more vivid.
