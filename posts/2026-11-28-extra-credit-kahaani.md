# Extra Credit: Kahaani

The entire room was only a few grandmas and me. Not sure what I expected to be the target audience of this event.

Bollywood movies always have a bad rep. I myself have a habit of associating anything Indian with low quality.  
With movies specifically, Indian movies are typically associated with the stereotype of unironically being overly corny, and [overusing special effects to the point of being comical][bollywod-movie-effects].  
Before this, that was all I know about Bollywood.

![kahaani-poster]

\[Spoiler Alert!\]
The movie opens with a segment showing a terrorist attack on a train, killing hundreds of people. 2 years later, a pregnant woman (the protagonist) went to India from the UK, looking for her missing husband, who hasn't been responding to her calls for 2 weeks. She went around the city finding help from everyone, from the police, to random hotel owners. No one has ever heard of her husband's name, until a woman from the company the husband supposedly works at notice he looked like an employee with a different name (Milan Damji). As the story unfolds, she eventually manages to get to Milan Damji, who turns out to be the terrorist behind the train attack 2 years ago. She then reveals that her pregnancy is fake and kills Milan Damji. Turns out her husband died 2 years ago in the attack and the entire story is a plot for revenge.

I did not have high expectations going in, but I did not go in expecting it to be bad, either.  
The first 10 minutes did not inspire confidence. The few freeze effects in the opening scences weren't as bad as the stereotype made them out to be, but they aren't good. 
Before going into the bad, I will probably talk about the good first.

## The good

The twist genuinely took me by surprise. My expectations until that point has gotten so low, I didn't expect anything else but a typical reveal at that point, where it turns out her husband is actually Milan Damji and has been secretly living his life as a double agent, like Ethan Hunt in Mission Impossible, or something like that. Her revealing her fake pregnancy and killing Milan Damji was genuinely a twist I didn't expect.

## The bad

Unfortunately, the ending's surprise factor is the only saving grace of the movie for me. Everything else can only sadly be described as mediocre. The overall plot isn't something groundbreaking or that original. The cinematography is pretty subpar. The moment-to-moment development does not leave me on the edge of my seat.

Sometimes, "bad" movies (maybe due to low budget, poor visuals, cliche,...) can be entertaining nevertheless.  
Other times, some movies are so bad they circle around to become entertaining due to its sheer absurdity. One that you don't "laugh with", but "laugh at". Like the Minecraft Movie. The kinds of movies where you can genuinely see yourself watching with your friends with the sole reason is to make fun of how bad it is.  
This movie is neither of that. It was just painfully mediocre.

Overall, just a generally forgettable experience.
